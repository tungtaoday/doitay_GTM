## Mục tiêu
Lấy 1 piece of content tốt → tạo 5-10 pieces cho các platforms khác.

## Repurposing Matrix

### From Twitter Thread →
- Instagram carousel (1 tweet = 1 slide)
- LinkedIn article (expand each tweet)
- Facebook post (combine into long-form)
- YouTube Short script (key points)
- Email newsletter (with added context)

### From Instagram Reel →
- YouTube Short (reformat)
- Facebook Reel (same, adjust caption)
- Twitter thread (key points as text)
- LinkedIn post (professional angle)

### From Long-form Content →
- 5-10 tweets (key insights)
- 3-5 Instagram carousels (frameworks/lists)
- 1-2 Reels (best hooks)
- LinkedIn article (professional angle)
- Email series (1 insight per email)

## Process
1. Identify top 20% performing content (by CMF score)
2. Extract core value nuggets
3. Adapt format and tone for target platform
4. Adjust hook for platform culture
5. Schedule across platforms (stagger, don't dump all at once)

## Output format
