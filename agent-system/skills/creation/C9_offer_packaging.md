## Mục tiêu
Package CVP thành irresistible offer.

## Offer Components
1. **Core Offer**: Sản phẩm/dịch vụ chính
2. **Bonuses**: Giá trị thêm tăng perceived value
3. **Guarantee**: Giảm risk cho buyer
4. **Urgency**: Lý do mua ngay (không fake scarcity)
5. **Price Anchor**: So sánh với alternatives

## Pricing Strategy
- **Cost-plus**: Cost + margin (simple, weak)
- **Value-based**: Price = % of value delivered to customer
- **Competitor-based**: Match or undercut
- **Penetration**: Low price to acquire, increase later

## Tier Design (if applicable)
- Tier 1 (Basic): Core feature only — low price, high volume
- Tier 2 (Pro): Core + extras — sweet spot, most revenue
- Tier 3 (Premium): Everything — high price, low volume, signals value
